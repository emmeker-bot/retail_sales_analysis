**Key Business Insights**





**Top \& Bottom Regions:**



Highest Revenue: 



Lagos generated ₦7,260,000.00 (29.9% of total company revenue).



Lowest Revenue: Kano generated ₦3,980,000.00 despite recording the same transaction count (8) as Lagos, driven down by lower average item unit costs.



**Top Product:**



Laptops were the highest revenue driver, yielding ₦6,850,000.00 across 7 transactions.



**Sales Rep Volume:**



John and Mary tied for top productivity by transaction count, closing 7 sales transactions each.



**Customer Segment Performance:**



Corporate Clients dominate revenue generation, accounting for ₦15,110,000.00 (62.2%) of total sales versus ₦9,180,000.00 (37.8%) from Individual retail customers.



**Temporal Trends:**

Peak sales occurred in May 2026 with ₦5,795,000.00 in total sales volume.



High-Value Transactions:



Exactly 8 transactions met or exceeded the ₦1,000,000.00 threshold, with Abuja leading in high-ticket orders.







**Strategic Recommendations for Management**





1. Prioritize Corporate Accounts: Corporate clients generate 62.2% of overall revenue

despite taking up fewer transactions. Create dedicated corporate account managers,

tailored bulk discount tiers, and SLA contracts for high-ticket items like Laptops and Phones.



2\. Re-evaluate Category Strategy in Kano: Kano generated the lowest revenue (₦3.98M)

despite having 6 transactions. Kano's product mix is heavily tilted toward low-margin

items (Chairs, Desks). Introduce bundles pairing office furniture with electronics

(e.g., Laptop + Desk packages) to elevate average order value in the Northern market.



3\. Capitalize on Mid-Year Buying Cycles: May represented peak sales activity (₦5.795M).

Prepare targeted inventory planning and marketing pushes starting in mid-April to prevent

stockouts during peak quarter-two buying cycles.
